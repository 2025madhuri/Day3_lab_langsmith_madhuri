# Domain Selection

## Chosen Domain

Restaurant Customer Support

## Document Sources

The dataset is based on realistic restaurant customer service interactions including reservation requests, allergen inquiries, customer complaints, menu recommendations, delivery issues, and customer feedback.

## What Makes a Good Example

A good example contains:

- Customer input/question
- Expected response or reference answer
- Clear customer service objective

The examples should test empathy, professionalism, accuracy, safety awareness, and customer satisfaction.
